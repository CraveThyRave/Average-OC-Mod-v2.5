C: Thorns Fah Mix was a very, very interesting song to create. It took a while to make due to important real life events, but I was very happy to work on it when I was able. My favorite part of composing this was definitely the vocals. I love doing vocals. Slightly related, it is very surprising to me that a lot of mods (and even the original game) don’t handle Spirit's vocals differently from Senpai's… I wish more fan-songs did that. I think it sounds nice.


D: The Rosebud of the highschooler cracks open, a Thorny and vile trapped soul reveals itself. Of course, Fah doesn't grasp the full story, but she was in mortal danger.
Director: The fake timebar gimmick is one I'd been itching to use this way for a while and I'm very happy I got to here. Truth be told there were more expansive concepts for this song but they got scrapped to get the song out.
VA: Spirit's voicework was extremely fun... I loved dealing with the chromatic, among everything else. He got to shine so well here... Mostlys.
Artist: A hell of a lot less work than the rest of the week for a pretty good payoff, his little glitch animation was heavily inspired by New madness and Tricky's little Glitchout when singing multiple notes, and his alts represent more of a sharp bird--but otherwise, there's not much to say about him, fun little guy. Though, there were actually graphics made for an entirely different take of the song, where Fah was moreso taken over by spirit in his attempt to escape, before inevitably failing due to her willpower (and her mom saving her) you tell me whats cooler, but I preferred this (#firstdudetoFCthorns).
